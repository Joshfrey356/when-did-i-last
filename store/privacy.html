Last Time

Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Short version: Last Time doesn’t collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The things you track, the dates you log, and any notes you write are saved only on your iPhone. They are included in your iPhone’s own iCloud or computer backup if you have that turned on, and that backup is controlled by Apple and by you, not by us.

What we collect

Nothing. Last Time has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. We never see your data.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No information is sent anywhere to deliver them.

Backups you export

If you use “Export backup,” the app creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes is up to you.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we’ll update this page and the effective date above.
