## Description
When did I last change the furnace filter? Call my mom? Get a haircut? Rotate the tires?

Last Time answers that in one glance. Add the things you always lose track of, tap once whenever you do them, and the app keeps count for you.

ONE TAP TO LOG
Tap the circle when you do something. Did it last week and forgot to log it? Long-press to pick the day.

GENTLE NUDGES
Set how often each thing should happen: every week, every 3 months, every year, or your own custom number of days. You get one quiet reminder in the morning when something is due. Never more than one a day.

SEE HOW LONG IT’S BEEN
Every item shows exactly how many days it’s been, what’s overdue, and what’s coming up next.

SPOT YOUR RHYTHM
See every time you’ve done something, the gaps in between, and how often you actually do it. If you always end up changing the filter every 70 days, Last Time will suggest that as your reminder.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone and in your iCloud device backup. Export a backup file anytime.

• Health: dentist, haircut, eye exam, physical
• People: call Mom, date night, check in on a friend
• Pets: flea meds, grooming, vet visits
